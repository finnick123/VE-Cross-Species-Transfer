# Reading map

- SATURN combines expression with protein-language-model embeddings and avoids restricting integration to one-to-one orthologs: https://doi.org/10.1038/s41592-024-02191-z
- SAMap iteratively combines sequence homology and manifold alignment across distant species: https://elifesciences.org/articles/66747
- scArches performs query-to-reference transfer and reports important limits in cross-species mapping: https://doi.org/10.1038/s41587-021-01001-7
- scIB establishes multi-metric evaluation of integration rather than relying on one visualization: https://doi.org/10.1038/s41592-021-01336-8
- The extended mouse gastrulation atlas provides a relevant developmental reference through early organogenesis: https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-023-05838-9
- A rabbit gastrulation atlas demonstrates both conserved programs and the need for explicit cross-species stage reasoning: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7618511/
- Comparative organ developmental transcriptomes show that timing relationships are organ-specific: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6658352/
