# Suggested Community Resource submission

**Title:** VEC Cross-Species Transfer Lab

**URL:** https://github.com/finnick123/VE-Cross-Species-Transfer

**Description:** A benchmark for testing whether embryonic representations transfer across species. It measures gene attrition, cross-species cell-type retrieval, within-type species mixing, developmental-order preservation and unknown-state rejection through a simple embedding CSV interface and deterministic tests.
